      Everything here is television. The catalogue holds no films, so format means the kind of programme.

      How this works

      Every pick is scored against the shows you rated: plot wording, theme signals read from summaries, and genre tags. Your closest match pulls harder than the rest, and anything close to what you disliked is pushed down.

      A match score is similarity, not a promise you’ll enjoy it. Themes come from English summary text, so titles with thin descriptions match on genre alone.
