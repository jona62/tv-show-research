          How Couchside works

          Every row is built from the shows you rate. Love this pulls hardest, I like this pulls a little less, and anything close to what you marked Not for me is pushed back. Rows called Because you loved follow one show at a time.

          A match compares a show with your single best pick, so 99% is as close to your taste as anything gets. It measures how alike the stories, themes and genres are, not a promise you will enjoy it.

          The catalogue is a TVmaze snapshot of 89,594 series from 2026-09-07. Cast, seasons, episodes, backdrops and where a show streams or airs are fetched from TVmaze when you open a title, and posters load from TVmaze's image server.

          Trailers come from KinoCheck, which has official ones for most recent shows, and play in YouTube's no-cookie player only when you press play; their thumbnails load from YouTube's image server. Age ratings are the US ratings iTunes lists for the shows it sells, so shows iTunes does not sell have none. Service icons are fetched through DuckDuckGo by this server. Where to watch shows where a show first streams or airs; it may stream somewhere else in your country.

          Your ratings and My List stay in this browser. There is no account, and nothing about you is stored on a server. Nothing plays here: open a title on TVmaze to see where it airs.
