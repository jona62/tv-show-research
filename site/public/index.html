    What the numbers mean

    The model compares plot text, theme keywords, and genres. It combines your average positive match with your closest favorite, then subtracts a similarity penalty for disliked shows. Scores are clipped at zero.

    “Loved,” “Liked,” and “Somewhat liked” have positive weights of 1, 0.7, and 0.35. Neutral shows are only excluded. Disliked shows contribute to the penalty. These are adjustable heuristics, not a trained predictor of enjoyment.

    Search covers all 89,594 shows in this snapshot, including international shows, animation, documentaries, and reality TV. Recommendations require a known premiere on or before the snapshot and at least 15 summary words or one genre. Entries with sparse data are labeled; missing features contribute zero similarity. The dataset and its text features are refreshed manually; changing your list only recomputes the analysis.

    The model uses 40,000 text terms, 28 genres, and 32 theme signals. Theme rules use English words in catalog summaries, including summaries of international shows; untranslated text may have weaker coverage. Network country is not necessarily production origin or streaming availability.

    Themes miss pacing, performances, and character arcs. More ratings help describe your taste but do not turn similarity into a guarantee.
